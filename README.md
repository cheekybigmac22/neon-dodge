# AFTERIMAGE: LAST DRIVE

An original 2D horror-survival driving game. There are no weapons: keep your car on an impossible road, survive the time slips, and stay ahead of the thing that is learning to follow you.

## How to play

- Steer with the left/right arrow keys or A/D.
- On a touch screen, drag to steer.
- Drive as far as possible. The farther you get, the more the road corrupts and the more creatures appear.
- The **Reduce motion effects** option tones down screen shake, glitches, and time-slip flashes.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.
